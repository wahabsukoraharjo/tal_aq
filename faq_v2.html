Governance & Integrity Rules
Rules applied across Process Master, connectors, dashboard and future systems.
Decision completeness

Every decision requires at least two branches, a next-action owner and a destination process.

Pending control

Hold, Pending and Dipertimbangkan require reason, owner, next action and follow-up date.

Identity continuity

Person ID remains the master identity; Candidate ID is linked to Employee ID after hiring.

Status separation

Job Order, Application, Tele Interview, Employment, Assignment, Learning and Contract statuses remain separate.

Core distinctions

Disarankan ≠ Ready for Client Submission; Ready for Matching ≠ Ready for Deployment; Deployed ≠ Billable.

Replacement traceability

Every case carries Original Job Order ID, previous talent reference and Replacement Case ID.

Restricted data

Psychometric details, salary, payroll nominal, billing rate, margin and sensitive feedback use role-based access.

End states

Every path ends in Active, Closed, Talent Pool, Former Employee or Archived; no process is allowed to hang.
